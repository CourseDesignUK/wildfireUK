About the Fire Risk Viewer

What This App Does

Provides pan-European wildfire danger tracking modeled directly on the European Forest Fire Information System (Copernicus EFFIS). It calculates the Canadian Forest Fire Danger Rating System (CFFDRS) suite, monitors real-time satellite fire hotspots, maps CAMS smoke plumes, and applies topographic slope multipliers.

Scientific Data Sources

  • Copernicus EFFIS: Fire Weather Index (FWI) classifications and official WMS raster feeds.
  • Open-Meteo API: Live high-resolution meteorological telemetry (surface temperature, 10m wind speed, relative humidity, and precipitation).
  • NASA FIRMS: Real-time VIIRS (375m) and MODIS satellite thermal anomalies detecting active fire radiative power.
  • CAMS (Copernicus Atmosphere): Smoke plume modeling and particulate concentrations ($PM_{2.5}$, $PM_{10}$, $CO$).
  • Copernicus CLMS: Corine Land Cover vegetative fuel classifications.

Configuring the NASA FIRMS API Key

Open-Meteo, EFFIS WMS, and CAMS operate without registration. To connect live NASA satellite fire observations directly:

  1. Request a free key at NASA Earthdata FIRMS.
  2. Tap the key icon () in the top bar.
  3. Paste your 32-character key to save it to your local browser storage.
