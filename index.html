About Copernicus EFFIS Viewer

Coverage: UK • Germany • Cyprus

What this App Does

Provides mobile-first Canadian Forest Fire Danger Rating System (CFFDRS) calculations and EFFIS fire risk monitoring across the UK, Germany, and Cyprus. It calculates Fire Weather Index (FWI), Fine Fuel Moisture (FFMC), Initial Spread Index (ISI), and Drought Code (DC) mapped over OpenStreetMap or Google Satellite imagery.

Regional Biomes & Telemetry

  • United Kingdom: Upland heather moors, Molinia grasses, deep organic peatlands, and lowland gorse heaths.
  • Germany: High-risk sandy pine monocultures (Brandenburg), Harz spruce beetle dieback zones, and Bavarian montane forests.
  • Cyprus: Extreme Mediterranean fire climates, Troodos Calabrian pine (Pinus brutia), garrigue, and maquis shrublands.

Data Sources

  • Copernicus EFFIS: CFFDRS danger classes, index formulations, and threshold standards.
  • Open-Meteo V1 API: Real-time 2m temperature, relative humidity, 10m wind speed, and precipitation.
  • NASA FIRMS: Near real-time thermal anomalies from VIIRS (S-NPP, NOAA-20/21) 375m and MODIS satellites.

Adding Your Free NASA FIRMS API Key

  1. Visit firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/map_key/.
